⚙️ Tech Stack & Engineering Philosophy

  • Backend Runtime: Python 3.12 with Flask serving RESTful API routes and Jinja2 templates.
  • Zero NPM/Build Tool Dependencies: The frontend utilizes native Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), standard Semantic HTML5, and CSS Custom Properties. You never need to run Webpack, Vite, or npm install to modify or deploy the application.
  • No SQL Database Requirement: Complete state persistence is achieved through structured JSON disk stores in data/ with automated backup and recovery mechanisms.
  • Tournament Accuracy: Follows official Gen 9 competitive standards (VGC Regulation H and Smogon OU).

⚙️ Backend Modular Engines (utils/)

⚔️ utils/battle_engine.py 3,126 Lines • Core Battle State Machine & Dynamic Registries

Purpose: Governs all in-battle mechanics according to official Pokémon Generation 9 competitive rules and the unified Omni Ring standard.

Core Data Classes & State:

  • PokemonState: Tracks active HP, stat stages (-6 to +6), non-volatile persistent status (burn, freeze, paralysis, sleep, poison), volatile conditions (confusion, flinch, infatuation), item availability, Terastallization state, Mega Evolution state (strictly validated by held Mega Stone), Gigantamax state (3-turn counter, 1.5× HP boost), PP, choice_locked_move, disguise_busted, can_fake_out, and charging_move.
  • FieldState / BattleSimulator: Tracks global weather (rain, sun, snow, sandstorm with 5-turn counters), terrain (grassy, electric, psychic, misty with 5-turn counters), and Trick Room (5 turns).
  • BattleSide: Tracks team roster, active index, side screens (Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil with remaining-turn countdowns), Tailwind (4 turns), entry hazards (Stealth Rock, Spikes layers, Toxic Spikes, Sticky Web, Steelsurge), and lingering 4-turn G-Max fields (Wildfire, Cannonade, Vine Lash, Volcalith).

Key Battle Methods & Mechanics:

  • calculate_damage(attacker, defender, move_name, is_crit) -> (int, dict): Computes exact damage using the Gen 9 formula factoring Base Power, stat stages, weather boosts, terrain multipliers, STAB, type effectiveness (including Freeze-Dry on Water), Multiscale (50% cut at 100% HP), Disguise absorption (ignoring Dragon moves on Fairy), Supreme Overlord (+10% per fainted ally), screens mitigation, and physical burn penalty.
  • calc_spread_multiplier(move_name, targets_count) -> float: Applies the official 0.75× damage scaling to spread attacks (Muddy Water, Bleakwind Storm, Heat Wave, Earthquake) when multiple defenders are present, or 1.0× full damage if a solo defender remains.
  • check_priority_block(attacker, defender, move_name) -> bool: Validates that grounded Pokémon targeted by priority moves in Psychic Terrain are completely immune.
  • execute_trick_switcheroo(user_poke, target_poke): Atomically swaps held items between user and target, dynamically releasing or binding Choice locks while enforcing Mega Stone item security.
  • get_mega_species_for_item() -> Optional[str]: Dynamically checks MEGA_STONE_MAP to verify that the Pokémon is holding its designated Mega Stone (or knows Dragon Ascent for Rayquaza) before allowing Mega Evolution, strictly dictating forms (e.g. Charizardite X vs Y, Lucarionite vs Lucarionite Z, Absolite vs Absolite Z).
  • execute_turn(action1, action2): Sorts actions via Priority Brackets (+5 to -7), Trick Room inversion, and dynamic same-turn Speed recalculation (speed changes from Tailwind or Mega Evolution take effect immediately).
🤖 utils/battle_ai.py 370 Lines • Decision & Prediction Engine

Purpose: Powers the AI computer opponent from Difficulty 1 to 100.

Heuristic Algorithm:

  • Damage Utility: Computes raw percentage damage against opponent's current HP. Awards +120 bonus for lethal Knockouts.
  • Weather Awareness: Recognizes that Hurricane and Thunder have 100% accuracy under Rain, and Blizzard has 100% accuracy under Snow.
  • Level 100 Champion Lookahead: Leverages Minimax depth to predict switch-ins. Evaluates opponent's bench for resistances and pre-emptively selects super-effective coverage or setup moves.
🧠 utils/battle_knowledge.py Reinforcement Machine Learning Cache

Purpose: Persistent memory and telemetry engine for Championship AI. Bridges post-game battle analytics with future AI action selection via data/battle_knowledge_cache.json.

Core Subsystems & Functions:

  • load_battle_knowledge() -> Dict[str, Any]: Loads persistent knowledge cache from disk with fallback to factory defaults.
  • save_battle_knowledge(data: Dict[str, Any]): Safely serializes knowledge cache to disk via atomic UTF-8 JSON formatting.
  • record_battle_outcome(battle: BattleSimulator) -> Dict[str, Any]: Mines the terminal battle log for key knockout events, maps decisive killer moves to victim species, updates exponential moving average win-rates ($WR \leftarrow WR \times 0.8 + Target \times 0.2$), and writes human-readable match debriefs.
  • reset_battle_knowledge() -> Dict[str, Any]: Re-initializes both disk cache and active memory to pristine zero-game state.

💾 utils/cache.py 1,280 Lines • Multi-Tier Dynamic Storage & Discovery Engine

Purpose: Governs in-memory and disk cache synchronization, atomic writes, debounced disk flushes, and dynamic data discovery.

Key Functions & Architecture:

  • get_mega_stone_map(force_refresh=False) -> dict: Dynamically inspects items_cache and pokemon_cache for Mega Stones and forms, resolving species and variants via regex parsing and persisting to data/mega_stones_cache.json.
  • get_species_gmax_moves_map(force_refresh=False) -> dict: Dynamically loads and updates signature G-Max moves and secondary effects to data/gmax_moves_cache.json.
  • get_move_overrides_map(force_refresh=False) -> dict: Loads competitive move definitions from data/move_overrides_cache.json.
  • Reentrant Concurrency: Implements threading.RLock() across all cache structures to prevent deadlocks during nested synchronization and telemetry reads.
  • Reactive PokéAPI Ingestion: Fetching newly discovered items or refreshing caches automatically triggers live updates to the Mega Stone and G-Max registries.

Official Gen 9 Damage Formula

DAMAGE CALCULATION FORMULAPYTHON IMPLEMENTATION
# 1. Base Damage Calculation
base = math.floor(math.floor(math.floor(2 * level / 5 + 2) * power * atk / defn) / 50) + 2

# 2. Doubles Spread Move Scaling (0.75x if multiple targets alive, 1.0x if solo)
if is_doubles and is_spread_move and active_opponents_count > 1:
    base = math.floor(base * 0.75)

# 3. Weather Modifiers (via get_effective_weather())
if weather == "rain" and move_type == "water": base = math.floor(base * 1.5)
if weather == "rain" and move_type == "fire":  base = math.floor(base * 0.5)
if weather == "sun"  and move_type == "fire":  base = math.floor(base * 1.5)
if weather == "sun"  and move_type == "water": base = math.floor(base * 0.5)

# 4. Critical Hit Modifier (1.5x)
if is_crit: base = math.floor(base * 1.5)

# 5. Random Damage Variance (85% to 100%)
variance = random.uniform(0.85, 1.0)
damage = math.floor(base * variance)

# 6. Same Type Attack Bonus (STAB)
if move_type in attacker.types:
    damage = math.floor(damage * (2.0 if attacker.tera_type == move_type else 1.5))

# 7. Type Effectiveness Multiplier (including Freeze-Dry on Water)
multiplier = get_type_multiplier(move_type, defender.types, move_name=move_name)
damage = math.floor(damage * multiplier)

# 8. Physical Burn Penalty (halved unless attacker has Guts)
if attacker.status == "burn" and category == "physical" and attacker.ability != "guts":
    damage = math.floor(damage * 0.5)

# 9. Defensive Screens Mitigation (Reflect / Light Screen / Aurora Veil)
if category == "physical" and (defender_side.reflect or defender_side.aurora_veil):
    damage = math.floor(damage * (0.66 if is_doubles else 0.5))
elif category == "special" and (defender_side.light_screen or defender_side.aurora_veil):
    damage = math.floor(damage * (0.66 if is_doubles else 0.5))

# 10. Signature Abilities: Multiscale & Disguise
if defender.ability == "multiscale" and defender.hp == defender.max_hp:
    damage = math.floor(damage * 0.5)
if defender.ability == "disguise" and not defender.disguise_busted and attacker.ability not in ("mold-breaker", "teravolt", "turboblaze"):
    damage = 0  # Disguise absorbs the hit, then busts for 1/8 max HP recoil

# 11. Supreme Overlord Attack Boost (+10% per fainted ally)
if attacker.ability == "supreme-overlord":
    damage = math.floor(damage * (1.0 + 0.10 * attacker_side.fainted_allies_count))

🎯 Accuracy Engine & Weather Bypasses

Move accuracy resolution in check_move_accuracy() follows official competitive Pokémon rules:

  • Rain Accuracy Bypass: In Rain, Hurricane and Thunder bypass accuracy checks and cannot miss (100% accuracy).
  • Snow Accuracy Bypass: In Snow or Hail, Blizzard bypasses accuracy checks and cannot miss (100% accuracy).
  • Harsh Sun Reduction: In Harsh Sunlight, Hurricane and Thunder accuracy plummets to 50%.
  • Weather Suppression (Cloud Nine & Air Lock): If any active Pokémon on the field possesses Cloud Nine or Air Lock, all weather effects are disabled, restoring Hurricane and Thunder to their base 70% accuracy.
  • No Guard Ability: Guarantees 100% accuracy for all attacks performed by or directed at the Pokémon.
  • Never-Miss Attacks: Flower Trick, Kowtow Cleave, Aura Sphere, Aerial Ace, and Clear Smog inherently bypass accuracy rolls.

⏱️ Turn Ordering & Priority Brackets

Every turn in the simulator resolves actions through strict competitive priority sequencing:

  1. Replacements & Items: Safe fainted replacements and items resolve before any moves occur.
  2. Omni Ring Activation: One of Terastallization, Mega Evolution, or Gigantamax triggers at the beginning of the turn before attack execution. Gigantamax scales Max HP by 1.5x and sets a 3-turn duration counter.
  3. Move Priority Brackets (+5 to -7):
    • +5: Helping Hand
    • +4: Max Guard (Dynamax status moves), Protect, Detect, Baneful Bunker, King's Shield
    • +3: Fake Out, Quick Guard, Wide Guard
    • +2: Extreme Speed, Feint
    • +1: Sucker Punch (damaging moves only), Aqua Jet, Bullet Punch, Mach Punch, Ice Shard
    • 0: Standard Physical, Special, and Status attacks
    • -1: Vital Throw
    • -3: Focus Punch
    • -4: Avalanche, Revenge
    • -5: Counter, Mirror Coat
    • -6: Roar, Whirlwind
    • -7: Trick Room
  4. Prankster Boost: Adds +1 priority to status moves, but fails against Dark-type opponents.
  5. Psychic Terrain Priority Blocking: Grounded Pokémon targeted by priority moves (+1 to +5 priority) take 0 damage and the move fails.
  6. Speed Tier Resolution: Ties in priority brackets are resolved by Speed stats (inverted when Trick Room is active). Speed ties use a 50/50 random roll.

💍 Omni Ring Architecture & Mega Evolution / Gigantamax Validation

The simulator future-proofs competitive play by unifying generational mechanics into the Omni Ring:

  • side.omni_used: Boolean flag per side guaranteeing that only ONE mechanic (Terastallization, Mega Evolution, or Gigantamax) can be activated per battle.
  • Strict Mega Stone Validation: Mega Evolution is strictly verified against the Pokémon's held item. The engine dynamically queries get_mega_stone_map() to ensure the correct Mega Stone is held (e.g. Charizard holding Charizardite X evolves into Charizard-Mega-X, Charizardite Y evolves into Charizard-Mega-Y; Rayquaza requires knowing Dragon Ascent; Mewtwo requires Mewtwonite X / Mewtwonite Y; and canonical upcoming variants including Mega Z are fully supported). Mega Stones are permanently attached competitive artifacts: they cannot be stripped by Knock Off, nor swapped via Trick or Switcheroo.
  • get_max_move_details(species, move_name): Dynamically maps base moves into their Max Move counterparts:
    • Max Guard: Any move in the status category automatically converts to Max Guard with priority: 4. Regular Protect only blocks 75% of Max Move damage (dealing 25% chip), while Max Guard blocks 100% of damage (unless bypassed by G-Max One Blow or G-Max Rapid Flow).
    • Standard Max Moves: Maps damaging moves into Max Strike (-1 Spe), Max Quake (+1 SpD), Max Knuckle (+1 Atk), Max Ooze (+1 SpA), Max Steelspike (+1 Def), Max Airstream (+1 Spe), Max Flare (sets Sun), Max Geyser (sets Rain), Max Lightning (sets Electric Terrain), Max Overgrowth (sets Grassy Terrain), and Max Hailstorm (sets Snow).
    • Signature G-Max Moves: Specific species override matching-type standard Max moves with exclusive effects dynamically mapped via get_species_gmax_moves_map() (e.g. Lapras → G-Max Resonance sets Aurora Veil; Charizard → G-Max Wildfire sets Sun; Corviknight → G-Max Wind Rage clears screens and hazards; Blastoise → G-Max Cannonade sets Rain; Urshifu → G-Max Rapid Flow and G-Max One Blow pierce Protect and Max Guard).
  • Duration & HP Scaling: Gigantamax multiplies both Current and Max HP by 1.5x, lasting for 3 active turns on the field or until the Pokémon switches out.

⚡ Dynamic Mid-Turn Speed Recalculation & Two-Turn Moves

Following official Generation 7/8/9 competitive standards, turn order is no longer locked in stone at the start of a turn:

  • Dynamic Mid-Turn Speed Recalculation: When a Pokémon executes an action that modifies speed tiers—such as Mega Evolving into a form with a higher Base Speed, activating Tailwind, or switching abilities—the engine instantly recalculates effective Speed before the next action executes. If the speed advantage shifts, the newly faster Pokémon will act immediately before slower combatants.
  • Semi-Invulnerable Two-Turn Moves: Moves such as Phantom Force, Shadow Force, Fly, Dig, Dive, and Bounce enter a semi-invulnerable charge phase on Turn 1 (e.g., vanishing into shadows or flying up high), avoiding all standard incoming attacks. On Turn 2, the attack strikes at full power. Furthermore, Phantom Force and Shadow Force automatically break through Protect, Detect, Spiky Shield, Baneful Bunker, and Max Guard, lifting the opponent's defensive barrier.
  • Crash Damage Recoil: High-risk jumping moves like High Jump Kick and Jump Kick enforce competitive crash mechanics. If the attack misses due to accuracy, or if the target protects itself with Protect / Detect / Max Guard, the attacker crashes and sustains 50% of their maximum HP as recoil damage.
  • Generation 9 Substitute Mechanics: Sacrifices 25% max HP to craft a protective decoy doll. Damaging attacks strike the substitute; if damage exceeds substitute HP, the substitute breaks with zero spillover damage to the bearer. Completely blocks direct status moves (Will-O-Wisp, Toxic, Thunder Wave, Spore, Taunt, Yawn, Attract, Trick) and secondary move effects (flinch, stat drops, burn/paralysis/freeze/poison, Knock Off item loss). Sound-based moves (Boomburst, Bug Buzz, Hyper Voice, Torch Song, Snarl) and the Infiltrator ability bypass substitute directly. Can be inherited via Baton Pass or Shed Tail.

The battle engine accurately processes competitive abilities and held items:

2. The Learning Phase (Post-Battle Telemetry Ingestion)

Whenever a battle concludes (triggered via api_simulator_step in app.py), the backend calls record_battle_outcome(battle) in utils/battle_knowledge.py:

  • Knockout Mining: The engine iterates through the battle_log, matching every faint event with the active Pokémon that dealt the blow (killer_species) and the specific move used (killer_move).
  • Matchup Heuristics Key: Each knockout generates or updates a directional pair key: "{killer_species}_vs_{fainted_species}".
  • Exponential Moving Average (EMA) Win-Rate Formula: Rather than simple raw ratios, the win rate for that specific matchup action updates according to:
    $$\text{WinRate}_{\text{new}} = (\text{WinRate}_{\text{current}} \times 0.8) + (\text{Target} \times 0.2)$$
    where $\text{Target} = 1.0$ if the killer's team won the overall match, and $0.0$ if the team lost. This ensures recent tactical adaptations have immediate influence while preventing single-match flukes from overriding solid historical samples.
  • Post-Game Strategic Debriefs: Synthesizes a high-level strategic debrief detailing the decisive final knockout and appends it to post_game_learnings (retaining the 20 most recent match debriefs).

3. The Inference Phase (In-Battle Move Scoring)

During live turns, when the AI selects its next action in utils/battle_ai.py, it evaluates whether to leverage empirical memory:

  • Difficulty Activation Gate: Knowledge heuristics are gated strictly to Difficulty ≥ 70 (Elite Four and Championship tiers). Low-difficulty AI intentionally ignores knowledge heuristics to remain accessible to beginner players.
  • Matchup Key Lookup: When AI active Pokémon ($P_{\text{AI}}$) faces an opponent active Pokémon ($P_{\text{Opp}}$), it queries:
    matchup_key = f"{P_AI.species}_vs_{P_Opp.species}"
  • Utility Score Amplification Formula: In evaluate_move_utility(), if candidate move $M$ matches the heuristic's recommended_action:
    $$\Delta \text{Score} = \text{WinRate} \times 40.0$$
    A move with a proven $75\%$ win rate against that species receives an immediate +30.0 point boost. A flawless $100\%$ win rate yields the maximum +40.0 point boost.
  • Omni Ring Synergy: Because Mega Evolution, Gigantamax, and Terastallization candidate moves are evaluated through evaluate_move_utility(), this $+40.0$ historical boost directly influences whether the AI triggers its Omni Ring to power through a known threat.

4. Cache Telemetry & Reset Endpoints

  • GET /api/simulator/knowledge: Exposes real-time telemetry, win/loss stats, and learned matchup heuristics to the UI and Developer Diagnostics Hub.
  • POST /api/simulator/knowledge/reset: Fully resets the knowledge cache back to factory defaults both in active server memory and on disk.

🐛 Documented Bugfixes & Lessons Learned

1. Hurricane & Thunder Accuracy in Rain

Bug: Hurricane and Thunder were missing during Rain due to a raw 70% accuracy check.
Fix: Implemented check_move_accuracy() and get_effective_weather() in battle_engine.py. Under Rain, both moves bypass accuracy checks completely unless suppressed by Cloud Nine or Air Lock.

2. Teambuilder Offensive Coverage Normalization

Bug: Moonblast and Torch Song defaulted to Normal-type physical attacks in teambuilder diagnostics.
Fix: Enriched api_pokemon_teambuilder_data with EXHAUSTIVE_MOVES_DATA, added client-side KNOWN_MOVES_DATA fallback, and added normalizeMoveSlug() to match title-case, hyphens, and spaces cleanly.

3. Fainted Replacement Turn Consumption

Bug: When a Pokémon fainted, the replacement entered and attacked on the same turn.
Fix: Added is_replacement=True flag in perform_switch(), preventing replacement Pokémon from executing an attack action on entry.

4. Ghost Immunity against Body Press

Bug: Body Press damaged Ghost-type Pokémon.
Fix: Strictly enforced $0\times$ type immunity before evaluating defense stat substitutions.

5. Sucker Punch & Fake Out Priority Restrictions

Bug: Sucker Punch damaged status moves, and Fake Out worked on turn 2.
Fix: Sucker Punch verifies that the target selected a damaging move; Fake Out enforces turns_active == 1.

6. Mimikyu Disguise & Dragonite Multiscale Protection

Bug: Dragonite took 93% damage from attacks despite full HP Multiscale; Mimikyu took full damage instead of busting Disguise.
Fix: In battle_engine.py, implemented multiscale check halving damage at 100% HP. Implemented disguise absorbing incoming damage to 0 on the first hit, setting disguise_busted=True, applying 1/8 max HP recoil, and properly bypassing on Mold Breaker, Teravolt, or Turboblaze.

7. Trick & Switcheroo Held Item Swapping & Choice Lock Binding

Bug: Using Trick did not swap held items, and Pokémon holding Choice items could switch moves freely.
Fix: Implemented execute_trick_switcheroo() in battle_engine.py. Swaps held items atomically between user and target, updates item_revealed, releases Choice Lock from the giver, and binds Choice Lock to the recipient on their next chosen move.

8. Psychic Terrain Priority Move Blocking

Bug: Sucker Punch, Fake Out, and other priority attacks damaged grounded targets in Psychic Terrain.
Fix: Added is_grounded() and priority validation in battle_engine.py. All priority moves (+1 to +5) targeting grounded Pokémon automatically fail with a terrain protection message.

9. Doubles Spread Move Damage Scaling (0.75× vs 1.0×)

Bug: Spread moves like Muddy Water and Earthquake dealt full damage in Doubles regardless of opposing count.
Fix: Added calc_spread_multiplier() in battle_engine.py applying official Gen 9 0.75× damage scaling when multiple opposing targets are active, and 1.0× when only a single opponent is left alive.

10. Freeze-Dry Super-Effective Effectiveness Against Water Types

Bug: Freeze-Dry was logged as "Not very effective..." against Water-type Pokémon like Urshifu.
Fix: Added explicit move_name parameter in get_type_multiplier() in type_effectiveness.py. When move is freeze-dry and target is Water, the multiplier is multiplied by 4 (turning 0.5× resistance into 2.0× super-effective, and 1.0× into 4.0× quad weakness on Water/Dragon or Water/Ground).

11. Electro Shot Single-Turn Execution in Rain vs 2-Turn Charging

Bug: Electro Shot did not boost Special Attack or charged for 2 turns even during Rain.
Fix: Implemented two-turn charging logic in battle_engine.py. On turn 1, raises user SpA by +1 stage. In Rain, charges and executes instantly in 1 turn (granting +1 SpA and damaging immediately).

12. Battle Match Setup Modal Syntax & Default Hidden State

Bug: In Match Setup modal, users could not click any team selectors, Cancel, or Start Battle due to an uncaught syntax error.
Fix: Corrected empty assignment matchTitle.textContent = ; in simulator.html. Added hidden class by default to #setup-modal so it starts hidden behind the launchpad splash and only opens upon explicit user action.

13. Dynamic Speed Recalculation Mid-Turn

Bug: Turn orders were static at turn initialization. Mega Evolving into a higher Speed tier or activating Tailwind did not update turn priority for the remainder of the active turn.
Fix: Implemented mid-turn speed re-evaluation in battle_engine.py using sort_turn_actions(). Whenever a Pokémon changes form (Mega Evolution) or alters team speed (Tailwind), the action queue reorders dynamically before the next action executes.

14. Jump Kick & High Jump Kick 50% Max HP Crash Recoil

Bug: High Jump Kick misses or Protect blocks registered as standard misses with no recoil to the attacker.
Fix: Added crash damage routine in battle_engine.py. Missing with High Jump Kick or Jump Kick (whether from accuracy RNG or being blocked by Protect, Detect, or Max Guard) inflicts 50% of the attacker's maximum HP as crash recoil.

Bug: Phantom Force executed instantly in a single turn without invulnerability and failed against Protect.
Fix: Implemented two-turn semi-invulnerable charge logic in battle_engine.py. On Turn 1, the Pokémon vanishes or charges in a semi-invulnerable state (dodging attacks). On Turn 2, the strike lands; Phantom Force and Shadow Force bypass and break through opposing Protect and Max Guard barriers.

Bug: Paradox Pokémon holding Booster Energy did not proc Protosynthesis or Quark Drive on Turn 1 entry without active weather or terrain.

Bug: Dragon-type attacks (such as Draco Meteor) targeting Fairy/Ghost Mimikyu could inadvertently bust Disguise despite dealing 0 damage.

Bug: Mega Stones could be stripped by Knock Off or swapped via Trick/Switcheroo, and Mega Evolution forms were not validated against held items.

33. Recoil Damage & Drain Healing Capping to Actual Damage Dealt

Bug: Recoil moves (e.g. Brave Bird, Flare Blitz, Head Smash, Double-Edge) and drain moves (e.g. Giga Drain, Drain Punch) computed recoil and HP restoration based on theoretical raw damage rather than the damage actually inflicted on the defender. For example, if a target had only 7 HP remaining, a 293 theoretical damage Brave Bird inflicted 96 HP recoil (33% of 293) on the attacker instead of the official 2 HP recoil (33% of 7).
Fix:

  • Actual Damage Inflicted Capping: In perform_move(), the damage inflicted is strictly capped by the defender's current HP ($\text{actual\_dmg} = \min(\text{opp\_poke.current\_hp}, \text{total\_dmg})$). The combat log and telemetry accurately report the real HP reduction and percentage (e.g. "Volcarona took 💥 -7 HP (4.3%)").
  • Proportional Recoil Calculation: Standard percentage recoil moves now calculate recoil strictly from the actual damage dealt ($\text{recoil\_dmg} = \max(1, \lfloor \text{actual\_dmg} \times \text{recoil\_ratio} \rfloor)$). Attacking a 7 HP target with Brave Bird now correctly inflicts only 2 HP recoil! Struggle remains the canonical exception, dealing fixed recoil equal to 25% of the user's maximum HP.
  • Substitute & Multi-Hit Recoil Capping: When striking a Substitute, recoil is calculated solely from the damage absorbed by the decoy (capped at the decoy's remaining HP) plus any spillover strikes dealt to the Pokémon.
  • Drain Healing Capping: Drain attacks (Giga Drain, Drain Punch, Horn Leech) restore HP equal to 50% of the damage actually inflicted on the target rather than theoretical overkill numbers.
  • Rock Head & Magic Guard Immunity: Added complete recoil immunity for Pokémon with Rock Head (e.g. Aerodactyl, Hisuian Arcanine) and Magic Guard (e.g. Clefable), which also negates Life Orb recoil.
